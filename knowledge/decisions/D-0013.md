# D-0013 — Nothing being made is spent on its own ingredients

Accepted 2026-09-27. Rusty, on pack 1.66.0 (Warehouse Manager 0.5.0): "there is a recursion issue
with the warehouse pooled inventory crafting. I am creating iron ingots, which it is doing by
using iron nuggets, which it is doing by breaking down other iron ingots."

## What was wrong

D-0012's cycle guard stopped an item from being *made* while the same item was being made higher
up, and it began each top-level cell with an empty path. So two gaps: the recipe clicked was never
on the path (`plan` was not told its result), and an item on the path could still be *taken from
what is on hand*. Clicking `iron_ingot_from_nuggets` with ingots in the chests and no nuggets: the
nugget cells were short, a nugget was made by `iron_nugget` (one ingot to nine), and that rule took
an ingot from the chests. The fill broke an ingot into nuggets and placed them to make the ingot:
no gain, a wasted click, and the book and EMI lit the recipe as craftable from ingots alone.

## The rule

`Expansion.plan` and `most` take the recipe's result. The items being made form a chain from that
result down; nothing on the chain is made again below itself, and **no cell takes an item that is
on the chain above the rule the cell belongs to**, neither from what is on hand nor by making it,
however deep (the top recipe's result included). Spending what is being made on its own
ingredients gains nothing: ingot to nuggets to ingot at best breaks even. A rule may still spend
**its own** result from what is on hand (a recipe like one X and a Y making two X gains an X).

Every caller names the result, the argument being required so none can forget it: the fill
(`Pooled.pull`), the shortage report (`Pooled.shortage`, now given the registries to read the
result), the vanilla book and EMI (`Craftables`, reading it through the client level).
`reachable` stays count-free and chain-free: it is the cheap over-approximation before a plan,
and the plan now refuses what it used to wave through.

## Verified

Reproduced before the fix, twice: a JUnit case on the planner (the old answer was the step
`nugget ← ingot`) and a GameTest clicking vanilla's recipe at a managed table with five ingots in
the chest, which on the old behaviour failed with "nothing placed: nuggets 9". After it: the
JUnit partitions (the ingot from nuggets with ingots and a block on hand; the same at any depth
while the path serves any other result; a rule spending its own result, at the top and below) and
the GameTest (nothing placed, made or drawn, the five ingots stay; with nine nuggets in the chest
the click fills from them and the ingots stay). Clean build, booth included; numbers in
`../PROJECT.md`.
