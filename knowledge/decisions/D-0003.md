# D-0003 — Chest items dropped into the manager furnish the building

Accepted September 22, 2026.

Rusty built a bare dirt hut, placed the manager and saw nothing happen: "There were no
existing chests, I expected them to be created and placed." The proposal they accepted:
rather than conjuring free chests, the manager places the chest items you give it, along
the walls, one per group it wants, a double for a group that needs the room, then labels
and fills them as it does for chests you placed yourself.

Where: each scan also records wall spots, floor cells reached by the walk that stand on a
full block, back onto a full block, have the opposite side open, and have no door beside
them; the nearest to the manager are tried first. Whether: the domain's `Furnishing`
plans the building with one more container and places it only when that labels another
group (up to the nineteen leaves) or gives a group at or over 90 percent full a second
container; the latter takes two chest items as a double chest beside that group. A chest
item the plan has no use for is routed and stored like any other item, so the manager is
never a place where chests disappear.

Each placement schedules a rescan, so the new container joins the plan, gets its sign and
receives its stacks through the existing paths. Verified by a GameTest on an empty house
(seven chests stood on the floor, backed by walls, signed on the open face, seven groups)
and a booth scene of a bare hut.
