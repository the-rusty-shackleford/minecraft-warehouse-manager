# D-0002 — Containers cluster by group

Accepted September 22, 2026.

Rusty, on the first evening with 0.1.0 in their warehouse: the manager "split apart 'Food
1/2' and 'Food 2/2'. Possibly other similarities. We should keep things logically grouped
together."

0.1.0 chose a group's containers by three keys: the previous label, how much of the group
the container already held, and distance from the manager block. A group that overflowed
into a second container therefore took the next-nearest free container to the *manager*,
which can be across the building from its first.

0.1.1 adds position to the planner's view of a container and clusters:

- A group's second and later containers are the free ones nearest the containers it already
  has (after the previous label, before held contents: moving a couple of stacks is cheaper
  than walking).
- A group's first container, when nothing held decides it, is the free one nearest the
  containers of its sibling groups (same parent), so Stone stands by Wood by Earth.
- Leftover containers join the fullest group beside its existing containers.

The previous-label key still comes first, so an established layout does not reshuffle on a
rescan. Tests pin each rule with containers laid out in a line.
