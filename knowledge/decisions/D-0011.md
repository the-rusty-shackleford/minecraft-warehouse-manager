# D-0011 — A recipe with gaps in its pattern fills from the chests

Accepted 2026-09-25. Rusty, on pack 1.63.0: a Ranged Weapons Mod receiver, "steel in hand,
redstone in the chests", "flashed red all around" at a warehouse table and did not fill, while
with everything in the inventory it worked. The report read as a mixed-source failure, and the
first two reproductions of that (a GameTest with a tag ingredient in hand and the other item in
the chest, both ways; an EMI booth step with sticks in hand and redstone in the chests) passed.
The receiver itself, in the booth with the pack's jar, did not: EMI counted it craftable and
sent the fill, and the server drew nothing.

The cause was never the split. The lower receiver is three steel over one redstone, a pattern
with two empty cells. Vanilla's craftability check, which the pooled fill asks for the item it
picked per ingredient, answers one item per cell of the pattern, and for an empty cell that item
is air. The fill handed the list to the domain's `Pooling.pull`, which counted air as an
ingredient needed twice that nobody holds, found zero crafts possible, drew nothing, and vanilla
then answered with its ghost recipe, every slot red. Every recipe the tests and the booth had
used (sticks, torches, a block of iron, a redstone torch) has no gap, so it never showed; every
shaped recipe with a gap (a bucket, a hoe, a bow, the receivers, Immersive Aircraft's engine)
never filled from the chests since D-0004, unless the inventory alone covered it.

Accepted: the fill skips the empty cells in vanilla's answer. Nothing else changes; the
domain's contract ("one entry per ingredient of one craft") was right and the adapter now keeps
it. Rusty's "with both in the inventory it worked" is vanilla filling from the inventory with
nothing to draw.

Also in this change: `Pooled.pull` logs one line per fill at INFO (what was wanted, what was
drawn, or why not), the line the README's diagnosing section now names, so the next report of a
recipe that will not fill can be read off the server log.

Verified by a GameTest: a bucket at a managed table with iron in the chest fills three iron
from the chest, and with one iron in hand draws the other two; the booth fills a bucket through
EMI with one iron in hand, and, when the pack's Ranged Weapons Mod and Metals and Materials jars
stand in `run/booth/mods`, the lower receiver itself with two steel in hand and the rest in the
chests. The mixed-source tests stay, since they pin what the report first suggested.
