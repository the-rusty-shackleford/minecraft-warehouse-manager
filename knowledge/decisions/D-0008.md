# D-0008 — A fill the building cannot cover says what is short

Accepted 2026-09-24. Rusty, at the warehouse table: "When I try to craft the Engine from
resources in my inventory, it prefills the recipe with red slot backgrounds and does not let me
craft it." The Engine needs a boiler; there was none in their inventory or in any chest. The
game had answered correctly, with a ghost recipe, but vanilla tints every ghost slot red, so
nothing said which part was missing. Rusty: "Yes do that."

When the pooled fill finds the player's inventory, the grid and the building's containers
together cannot cover one craft, the server now sends one chat line before vanilla's ghost:
"Can't fill Engine from here: short of 1 × Boiler." Ingredients with the same alternatives are
counted together, in the recipe's order; an ingredient that accepts several items (a tag such as
coal or charcoal) is named by the first, and counts any of them. The arithmetic is the domain's
`Pooling.shortfall`, greedy over the ingredients in order, so where two different ingredients
accept the same item the later one is the one reported short; a hint, exact for the common
case. Chat, not the action bar: the action bar sits behind the crafting screen.

Nothing changes for a table outside a managed building, or for a player the manager does not
let draw: vanilla's ghost, as before.

Verified by JUnit over the domain (covered, short by one and several, alternatives, a shared
item, bad input) and a GameTest: a torch at a managed table with sticks in the chest and no
coal is short of one coal-or-charcoal, the message names "Torch" and "1 × Coal", the placement
places and draws nothing, and a coal in hand covers it.
