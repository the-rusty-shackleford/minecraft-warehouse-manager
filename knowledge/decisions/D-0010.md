# D-0010 — Clicking a recipe again piles the grid up from the chests

Accepted 2026-09-25. Rusty, on pack 1.63.0: "Clicking multiple times on a pooled warehouse
crafting recipe should multi stack the prefilled recipe if you have the components. For
example, i am trying to craft a lot of bullets. You craft 8 at a time. I cant just click the
bullets, say, 8 times to produce 64 in one fell swoop. I have to craft 8, click the bullet icon
again to prefill the recipe again, craft another 8, again and again."

Vanilla's recipe book already does what Rusty expects, from the inventory alone: its
`ServerPlaceRecipe.getStackSize` asks for one craft on a click, for one more than the grid
already holds per stack when the grid holds that recipe, and for the most the inventory allows
on a shift-click, each capped by the smallest stack the chosen ingredients make. The pooled
fill drew from the chests for exactly one craft on a plain click, whatever the grid held, so a
second click found nothing spare in the inventory and vanilla placed nothing.

Accepted: the pooled fill asks vanilla's question before it draws. `Pooling.wanted` is that
rule, pure and pinned by JUnit (a fresh grid, a grid holding one per stack, the stack cap, a
shift-click, nothing craftable, bad counts); `Pooled.pull` feeds it whether the grid holds the
recipe (`recipeMatches`), the smallest stack in the grid, the most the player and the building
together can craft, and the smallest max stack size among the chosen items. Everything
downstream is unchanged: the domain's `Pooling.pull` draws only the difference between what
that many crafts need and what the player already holds, vanilla clears the grid back into the
inventory and places the new count. EMI's fill goes through the same packet (D-0005), so a
click on EMI's recipe adds a craft too.

Verified by a GameTest: sticks at a managed table with sixteen planks in the chest, three
clicks leave two stacks of one, two and three planks in the grid with the chest debited each
time and nothing loose in the inventory; a shift-click then piles the rest on, sixteen planks
in the grid and the chest empty.
