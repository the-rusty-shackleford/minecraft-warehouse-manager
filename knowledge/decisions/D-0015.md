# D-0015 — The pooled fill counts, takes and gives what the player carries, their bags included

Accepted 2026-09-28. Part of Rusty's one change for every mod that looks at a player's inventory
("all things that see your inventory also see your backpack"): the Carried protocol
(`minecraft-carried`, its D-0001), nested in this jar.

The pooled fill runs just before vanilla's recipe-book placement, which Carried now lets count and
take from carried bags (its D-0004). So the fill must see them too, or it would draw from the
warehouse what the player already carries, or plan a step it then cannot take:

- **held** (what the player has towards a recipe) counts the inventory's main slots, the grid and
  every carried bag's storage cells: what vanilla's placement can take from (the offhand aside,
  as vanilla leaves it).
- **take** (one of each pick for a step the fill makes) looks in the inventory, then the grid,
  then the bags (`Carried.draw`, stores only).
- **give** (a step's result and remainders) and **draw** (the warehouse's stock pulled for the
  craft) go where a give goes, a bag included (`Carried.giveOrDrop` / `give`); only what fits
  nowhere is dropped. The index's "to inventory" withdrawal is the same give.

The client's recipe book counts the bags through Carried's hook on `fillStackedContents`, which the
book mixin here already calls; nothing to change there.

**EMI.** EMI counts from its first handler's input slots, and this mod's table handler stands first
(D-0005). Backpacks+ 0.6.0 adds its own table handler, EMI's with the worn bag's cells added (its
D-0033). As first built, both moved themselves to the front of EMI's list, so the one registered
last won: the release gate's run of the booth found Backpacks+'s first, and EMI at a managed table
counted no warehouse. Now Backpacks+ stands its handler just ahead of EMI's own, so the order is
this handler, Backpacks+'s, EMI's, whichever registers first; and this handler takes its input
slots from the handler behind it rather than fixing the inventory's and the grid's. At a managed
table EMI counts the inventory, the grid, the bag and the building; at any other table it behaves
as the handler behind it, bag included. Without Backpacks+ the handler behind is EMI's own.

Verified by `aFillDrawsOnTheBagsTheInventoryAndTheChests` with Backpacks+ 0.6.0 on the gametest
server: the coal only in a pocketed bag and the sticks in the chest make a torch (a coal from the
bag, a stick from the chest); a crafting table from one log only in the bag (the planks made first,
the log taken out of the bag, four planks placed). Failed on 0.6.0 (the bag's coal was not held).
The thirty earlier GameTests pass unchanged. The booth, with Backpacks+ 0.6.0 and EMI on its client:
the three handlers in that order at a managed table; a worn bag's two sticks counted by EMI beside
the building's; EMI's fill of a stone pickaxe through this handler takes the sticks out of the bag
and three cobblestone from the chests.
