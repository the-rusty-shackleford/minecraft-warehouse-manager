# D-0007 — The pooled fill works for recipes the player has not unlocked

Accepted 2026-09-24.

Rusty, at a warehouse table on pack 1.62.1: "I just tried crafting a propeller which only
needs iron, and I have iron in my chests but it did not even pre fill the recipe. That is a
bug."

The pooled fill (D-0004, D-0005) goes through the vanilla recipe-book placement packet, and
vanilla's server side refuses, silently, any recipe the player's recipe book does not contain;
`Pooled.pull` carried the same gate. The propeller is five iron ingots, but Immersive Aircraft
ships no recipe-unlock advancements at all, so a player who has never crafted a propeller by
hand has never unlocked it, and Rusty's book on the box indeed did not have it. Every
Warehouse Manager test and booth had awarded the stick recipe first, so the gate was never
exercised. EMI's own fill moves items by slot clicks and never asked the book, so this was a
regression for every modded recipe without an unlock.

Accepted: for a managed table where the player may draw, a recipe not in their book is
unlocked at the fill, with `awardRecipes`, exactly as crafting it by hand once would have
done; vanilla's placement then proceeds. Under `doLimitedCrafting`, where the game itself
refuses unknown recipes, nothing is unlocked and nothing is drawn.

Rejected: placing the grid ourselves to bypass the gate (a second copy of vanilla's placement
to keep in step); awarding every recipe the tally could craft when a table opens (noise, and
the book would fill with things the player never made).

Verified by a GameTest: a fresh mock player with the stick recipe locked fills two planks from
the chest and holds the recipe afterwards; with `doLimitedCrafting` on, a second fresh player
gets nothing and stays locked.
