# D-0004 — Crafting tables in the building draw on its containers

Accepted September 22, 2026.

Rusty: when crafting at a station in the same structure and filtering the recipe book by
what you can craft, "it should consider your pooled chest inventory", and crafting "should
deduct resources from your chests", "basically to make it way more convenient to craft".
The proposal they accepted: two halves, seeing and taking.

Seeing: the recipe book's craftable filter is computed on the client from the player's own
inventory, so when a player opens a crafting table whose position the manager's last walk
reached, the server sends that client a tally of the building's containers. A client mixin
on the recipe book's tally adds it while that table is open, and refreshes the book when a
new tally arrives.

Taking: vanilla fills the grid from the player's inventory. A mixin on the menu's recipe
placement first lets the domain's `Pooling` decide, from the item the game picked for each
ingredient, how many crafts the player plus the building can supply and what to draw; the
manager moves that from its containers, nearest first, into the player's inventory, and
vanilla proceeds. Crafting consumes it. Ingredients drawn but not crafted stay with the
player rather than teleporting back. A table outside the walls, or the inventory's own grid,
sees nothing.

These are the mod's first mixins: vanilla offers no hook on either side. Crafting tables
only; furnaces and the 2x2 grid can follow the same pattern.

Verified by a GameTest that crafts sticks at a table with the planks sitting in a warehouse
chest, checks the debit and the outside-table case, and a booth photo of the recipe book
lighting sticks with an empty inventory, then the grid filled from the chests.
