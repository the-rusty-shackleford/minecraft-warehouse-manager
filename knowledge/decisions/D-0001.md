# D-0001 — What a building is, and what the manager may touch

Accepted September 22, 2026.

Rusty acked the design preview with three calls and one clarification.

**A building is what a flood fill from the block reaches** through non-full-cube blocks, bounded
by solid blocks and by open sky. The sky rule means cells under an open atrium do not count;
Rusty judged that "fair enough" against the alternative, a pure cell budget, which leaks through
any open door. The budget (20,000 cells) remains as the backstop for caves and hangars.

**Existing chest contents are moved.** Rusty: "that's just a known side effect of consciously
placing down a manager block." No opt-out marker ships; the README says so plainly.

**A sign is conjured only when the container has no sign of its own**; one it has is rewritten
instead. Rusty set the rule as "within one block"; 0.1.1 narrowed it to attached to the
container's faces or standing directly above it, after Rusty's stacked chests showed two
signs each with half of them wrong: a sign between two stacked chests is within one block of
both, and the first chest processed took it. Signs cost nothing: consuming them would need the
warehouse to stock signs before it could label itself.

**Managed containers are a block tag** (`warehousemanager:managed`: chest, trapped chest, barrel),
so a datapack can add or remove container types. Rusty asked what "via the tag" meant; the answer
was that barrels get sorted and labelled like chests, and they stayed in.

Planner behaviour that falls out of the design rather than from a call: top groups first, the
smallest folded into Misc while they do not fit, the fullest group given a second container before
any group is split (at 90 percent), then the largest group split into its children while containers
remain; labels stick while their group still needs a container.
